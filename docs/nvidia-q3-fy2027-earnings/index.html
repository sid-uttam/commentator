Know this
The big storyline

First results without Jensen Huang

Huang resigned as CEO and left the board on 12 November, after allegations about his private life raised security concerns. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO while the board looks for a permanent replacement, and Harvey Jones became Nvidia's first-ever board chair. CNBC reports the board is not considering bringing Huang back.

Why it matters

Expect leadership questions to hang over the call. Watch whether the stock reacts more to the numbers or to the uncertainty.

NVIDIA Newsroom · CNN · Al Jazeera · CNBC
When

Results Tuesday after the US close, call at 5pm ET

Nvidia reports the quarter that ended 25 October. Written commentary from Colette Kress, now interim CEO as well as CFO, goes up at about 1:20pm PT, then the call starts at 2pm PT. Questions on the call are limited to analysts and institutional investors.

NVIDIA Investor Relations
Expectations

Wall Street expects about $109 billion in revenue

Third-party consensus puts earnings per share at $2.47 and revenue between $108.8 and $109 billion. These are analyst compilations, not company guidance. Last quarter Nvidia reported $96.22 billion in revenue and $2.22 per share.

InsiderFinance · Finance Calendar
The number that may matter more

Margins, not just the beat

Nvidia has told investors its gross margin will dip next quarter because memory chips cost more. So a clean beat on this quarter may not be what sets the share price.

Why it matters

Watch the guidance for next quarter as closely as this quarter's results.

What everyone's arguing about

Talking point 1Unresolved

Can Nvidia run without Huang?

Steady so far

Kress: "Our momentum is real, our demand is accelerating, our supply is growing." Analysts praised how she presented the quarter, and the stock rose during the call.

Open questions

Huang said he would be open to returning; the board says it does not intend to bring him back. A permanent CEO search is under way, with no timeline.

Kiplinger live updates · CNBC Investing Club
Talking point 2Watching

Margins: the dip this quarter

The 74.9% margin beat, but the guide for this quarter is 71–72%. That's in line with what analysts expected, so the market looked past it. The next report shows whether it holds.

CNBC · FinanceFeeds (citing Reuters)
Talking point 3Divided

Is the AI selloff over?

Shares had fallen about 32% from their 6 October peak by 13 November, and markets dropped sharply on results day. Before the report, Bernstein's Stacy Rasgon downgraded the stock to Market Perform. After it, shares rose about 4% in extended trading. One night doesn't settle it.
