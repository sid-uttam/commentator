Know this
The big storyline

First results without Jensen Huang

Huang resigned as CEO and left the board on 12 November, after allegations about his private life raised security concerns. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO while the board looks for a permanent replacement, and Harvey Jones became Nvidia's first-ever board chair. CNBC reports the board is not considering bringing Huang back.

Why it matters

Expect leadership questions to hang over the call. Watch whether the stock reacts more to the numbers or to the uncertainty.

NVIDIA Newsroom · CNN · Al Jazeera · CNBC
When

Results Tuesday after the US close, call at 5pm ET

Nvidia reports the quarter that ended 25 October. Written commentary from Colette Kress, now interim CEO as well as CFO, goes up at about 1:20pm PT, then the call starts at 2pm PT (2am Wednesday in Dubai). Questions on the call are limited to analysts and institutional investors.

NVIDIA Investor Relations
Expectations

Wall Street expects about $109 billion in revenue

Third-party consensus puts earnings per share at $2.47 and revenue between $108.8 and $109 billion. These are analyst compilations, not company guidance. Last quarter Nvidia reported $96.22 billion in revenue and $2.22 per share.

InsiderFinance · Finance Calendar
The number that may matter more

Margins, not just the beat

Nvidia has told investors its gross margin will dip next quarter because memory chips cost more. So a clean beat on this quarter may not be what sets the share price.

Why it matters

Watch the guidance for next quarter as closely as this quarter's results.
