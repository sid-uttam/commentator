The story in 20 seconds

GTA VI unlocks at midnight local time on Thursday 19 November, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. In Dubai that's midnight on Wednesday night. Preloads opened on 12 November. The game was delayed twice: first from 2025, then from May 2026.
