Beef · watch the intros

Hokit vs Bruce Buffer

Last month, on N3on's stream, Buffer called Hokit disrespectful and pointed to his White House comments about Michelle Obama. Hokit hit back on X, saying fighters with real criminal records get a pass while he's called not classy enough.

Why it matters: Hokit flips off opponents mid-fight — he did it to Freeman and Blaydes. Expect something when Buffer calls his name.
Essentially Sports · MMA Sucka · Heavy
The champ

Gane has never won the undisputed belt in a fight

Lost to Ngannou, then Jones. His Aspinall fight ended in a no contest after eye pokes. He took the interim belt by knocking out Pereira at the White House, then got promoted when Aspinall vacated. This is his first real defence.

The challenger

Who is Josh Hokit?

Unbeaten (10-0) and in a title fight after just four UFC bouts. Beat Curtis Blaydes in a Fight of the Year contender that set a heavyweight strike record, then beat Derrick Lewis on the White House lawn. Went in a +195 underdog.

Main event · Intros

Hokit flips off Bruce Buffer

As Buffer called his name, Hokit walked up and gave him the finger. A lot of the crowd cheered. Nobody had done that to Buffer before.

Why did that happen?
Payback. Last month Buffer called him disrespectful over his Michelle Obama comments. After the event, Buffer said he'd planned to slap Hokit's hand away, but Daniel Cormier talked him out of it.
